PRINCIPAL/ FOUNDER


Sandra Aiken’s journey into design is deeply
shaped by her journey through life — a path
woven across continents, cultures and rich
experiences. Drawing inspiration from her
European upbringing, time spent in South Africa,
and extensive global travels, Sandra brings a
layered and considered perspective to every
project. Now based on the beautiful Kapiti Coast
of New Zealand, her work reflects a thoughtful
blend of international influence and local
sensibility. ​

Her personal and professional experiences allow
her to intuitively understand each client’s vision,
guiding them towards spaces that feel both
refined and deeply personal. ​

With a Bachelor’s Degree in Design and a
reputation built over more than 20 years in the
industry, Sandra is an accomplished interior
designer known for her discerning eye and
collaborative approach. She offers her clients
access to an extensive network of trusted
suppliers, alongside exclusive products and
materials, ensuring every project is curated with
depth, quality and distinction.

Discover our process.


We create lux, inviting spaces, bespoke to the personalised requirements of
every client, from concept to completion.

Your journey with D for Design begins with a consultation and a visit with your
designer to view your home or commercial space. We will collaborate with you
to produce a design concept that encompasses your vision and your budget.

We will then source every item needed- including construction, flooring,
furniture pieces, artwork - each and every detail required to make your vision a
complete reality. We'll present these to you with mood boards, drawings and
samples. Lastly, your project will be professionally installed and styled with all of the
finishing touches. Welcome home to your rejuvenated space.
